"Default" in this data isn't a loss

The dataset's default flag means a missed payment next month. Most missed payments get caught up. Following accounts through roll rates (current → 30 days late → 60 → charge-off), only of missed payments become losses within 12 months. Treating the flag as a loss would overstate expected losses by about .
